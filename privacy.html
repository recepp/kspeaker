Privacy Policy for Kspeaker

Last updated: February 22, 2026

IMPORTANT: Kspeaker uses third-party AI services to provide English learning features. By using this app, you consent to sharing data with these services as described below.

1. Information We Collect and Share

1.1 Data Sent to Third-Party AI Service (Google Gemini AI)

What data is sent:

  • Voice Recordings: Your spoken English audio is converted to text and sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis
  • Text Messages: Any text you type in the app is sent to Google Gemini AI for conversation and feedback
  • Device ID: A unique identifier for your device (not personally identifiable)
  • Conversation Context: Previous messages in your current session for contextual responses

Who receives this data:

  • Google Gemini AI (provided by Google LLC) - For natural language processing and conversation generation
  • Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Why we send this data:

  • To provide real-time English conversation practice
  • To analyze your speech and provide grammar/pronunciation feedback
  • To generate contextually appropriate AI responses
  • To help you improve your English speaking and writing skills

Your consent:

  • When you first use the app, you will be asked to accept this data sharing
  • You can stop using the app at any time to discontinue data sharing
  • Voice recordings are processed in real-time and NOT permanently stored by us

1.2 Other Data We Collect Locally

  • Usage Data: App usage statistics, session duration, and feature interactions (stored locally)
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Settings: Your language preferences, notification settings, and theme choices

2. Third-Party Services We Use

2.1 Google Gemini AI (Primary Service)

  • Purpose: Natural language processing, conversation generation, English feedback
  • Data Shared: Voice transcriptions, text messages, device ID
  • Data Retention: Governed by Google's AI services terms
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy
  • Your Protection: Google provides enterprise-grade data protection and complies with GDPR, CCPA

2.2 Sentry (Error Monitoring)

  • Purpose: Crash reporting and error monitoring (helps us fix bugs)
  • Data Shared: Anonymous crash logs, device type, OS version
  • No Personal Data: We do NOT send your voice, messages, or personal info to Sentry
  • Privacy Policy: Sentry Privacy Policy

2.3 iOS Native Services

  • Speech Recognition: Apple's on-device speech-to-text (processed locally on your device)
  • Text-to-Speech: Apple's native voice synthesis (processed locally)
  • Notifications: Apple Push Notification Service (only if you enable reminders)

3. How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption: All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • No Permanent Storage: Voice recordings are processed in real-time and NOT stored on our servers
  • Device ID Only: We use a random device ID, NOT your name, email, or phone number
  • Minimal Data: We only collect what's necessary for the app to function

4. Data Retention

  • Voice Recordings: Processed in real-time by Google Gemini AI, not permanently stored by Kspeaker
  • Text Messages: Sent to AI for processing, not stored by Kspeaker
  • Conversation History: Stored locally on your device only (deleted when you clear app data)
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data retained for 90 days
  • Crash Logs: Anonymous error logs retained for 30 days

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Control Your Data

  • Stop Data Sharing: Uninstall the app to immediately stop all data sharing
  • Clear Local Data: Delete app data from iOS Settings → Kspeaker → Clear Data
  • Request Data Deletion: Email us at privacy@kartezya.com
  • Access Your Data: Contact us to request what data we have

5.2 Opt-Out Options

  • Notifications: Disable in app Settings or iOS Settings
  • Analytics: Currently collected anonymously (no opt-out needed for anonymous data)
  • AI Processing: Cannot opt-out (core app functionality requires AI)

6. Children's Privacy

Kspeaker is rated 4+ and suitable for children. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

  • We do NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do NOT require registration or email for basic app use
  • Parents can request deletion of any data by contacting us
  • Voice data is processed in real-time and NOT stored

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in different countries:

  • Google Gemini AI: Operates in Google's global data centers (GDPR compliant)
  • Sentry: US-based service (Privacy Shield certified)
  • All services comply with international data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA)

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be:

  • Posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date
  • Notified to you via in-app message for significant changes
  • Take effect immediately upon posting

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data:

  • Privacy Questions: privacy@kartezya.com
  • Data Deletion Requests: privacy@kartezya.com
  • General Support: support@kartezya.com

10. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1.1 and 5.1.2)
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation - EU)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act - USA)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act - USA)
